# Lesson 34: Aeronautical Decision Making, Hazardous Attitudes, and Risk Management
## PPL_PA_I_H_03 | ACS Elements: PA.I.H.K1, K2, K3

**RKP_01: 5 Hazardous Attitudes — Name, Pattern, Antidote**
1. ANTI-AUTHORITY: "Rules don't apply to me." → Antidote: "Follow the rules — they are usually right."
2. IMPULSIVITY: "Do something NOW!" → Antidote: "Not so fast — think first."
3. INVULNERABILITY: "It can't happen to ME." → Antidote: "It could happen to me."
4. MACHO: "I can handle this — watch me." → Antidote: "Taking chances is foolish."
5. RESIGNATION: "There's nothing I can do." → Antidote: "I'm not helpless — I can make a difference."
Get-There-Itis = Invulnerability + Impulsivity + Anti-Authority combined. Most accidents involve multiple attitudes.

**RKP_02: PAVE — Pre-Flight Risk Assessment**
P = PILOT: IMSAFE checklist (Illness, Medication, Stress, Alcohol, Fatigue, Eating).
A = AIRCRAFT: AVIATES inspections current, MEL, W&B, density altitude performance.
V = ENVIRONMENT: METARs/TAFs/PIREPs/SIGMETs, terrain, NOTAMs, airspace.
E = EXTERNAL PRESSURES: deadlines, passengers, costs, obligations. → Pre-commit criteria BEFORE flight; honor them without renegotiation.

**RKP_03: 5P Model — In-Flight Continuous ADM**
Plan, Plane, Pilot, Passengers, Programming — review every 30 min / each waypoint.
PLAN CONTINUATION BIAS: "We've come this far" — the most common fatal GA decision failure.
Antidote: expect to divert. Define divert criteria in advance and honor them automatically.

**RKP_04: IMSAFE and Alcohol Regulations**
14 CFR 91.17: 8 hours bottle to throttle (MINIMUM). BAC 0.04% maximum. "Under influence" at any time = illegal.
The 8-hour rule is a floor — not a clearance. High alcohol volumes need more than 8 hours.
IMSAFE: self-assess EVERY flight. If you cannot honestly say IMSAFE, you are not fit to fly.
Personal minimums: set YOUR minimums ABOVE the legal minimums. Honor them. Never negotiate them under pressure.

### Key Regulations

- 14 CFR 91.17 — Alcohol and drug prohibition; 8-hour bottle-to-throttle and 0.04% BAC limit
