# Lesson 22: Special Use Airspace — SUA, TFRs, and the D.C. SFRA
## PPL_PA_I_E_04 | ACS Element: PA.I.E.K3

### What This Lesson Covers
The six categories of Special Use Airspace (SUA), the mechanics and danger of TFR violations, how to legally check and contact controlling authorities for Restricted Areas and MOAs, and the Washington D.C. SFRA — the most enforced and most intercepted airspace restriction in the United States.

### The Four Knowledge Points

**RKP_01: The Six SUA Types — Entry Rules Chart**
| SUA Type | Entry Rule |
|---|---|
| Prohibited Area (P-xxx) | NEVER. No clearance possible. |
| Restricted Area (R-xxx) | ATC authorization required when active. |
| Warning Area (W-xxx) | No clearance needed (international waters). Exercise extreme caution. |
| MOA | VFR: legal at any time. Contact authority for status. IFR: ATC coordination needed. |
| Alert Area (A-xxx) | Legal VFR entry. Extreme vigilance required. |
| Controlled Firing Area | Not charted. Self-clearing. No restrictions. |

Depicted on sectional: Prohibited and Restricted = blue hatching. MOA = magenta hatching with label. Alert = magenta/blue outlined box.

**RKP_02: TFRs — NOTAM Only. Sectionals Cannot Show Them.**
Types: Disaster/Hazard (91.137), Space Launch (91.143), Presidential/VIP (91.141), Security (91.141), Airshow (91.145).
Presidential TFR: 10 NM inner no-fly ring. 30 NM outer ring requires Mode C.
TFRs are published ONLY via NOTAM and tfr.faa.gov. Sectional charts are 6-month printings — they cannot reflect active TFRs.
Consequence of TFR violation: military intercept, civil penalty, certificate action. Presidential TFR violations = highest enforcement priority.

**RKP_03: Restricted Area / MOA — Who Controls, How to Call**
Chart Supplement → SUA section → controlling agency + phone number.
Call before flight: 'Is [designator] active on [date/time] at [altitude]?'
If inactive: transit legally. If active or uncertain: route around.
Airborne option: ask enroute ATC for SUA status or contact military on published frequency.
A military intercept is the consequence of unauthorized entry into an active Restricted Area.

**RKP_04: D.C. SFRA — The Most Enforced Airspace in the U.S.**
30 NM SFRA: all VFR flights must complete FAA SFRA online course, obtain discrete transponder code from ATC, file flight plan, get ATC clearance, maintain constant radio contact.
15 NM FRZ (Flight Restricted Zone): no VFR civil operations without specific security authorization.
P-56A/B (Prohibited): directly over the White House/Capitol — absolute prohibition.
Multiple GA intercepts occur every year. Willful violation can result in criminal charges.

### Key Regulations
- 14 CFR 91.133 — Prohibited and Restricted Areas (civil aircraft restrictions)
- 14 CFR 91.137 — TFR: Disaster/hazard operations
- 14 CFR 91.141 — TFR: Presidential/security operations
- 14 CFR 91.143 — TFR: Space operations
- 14 CFR 91.145 — TFR: Aerial demonstrations
- 14 CFR 93.335/93.341/93.345 — D.C. SFRA requirements
