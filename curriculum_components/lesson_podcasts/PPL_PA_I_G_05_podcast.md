# Lesson 31: Pitot-Static and Vacuum Systems — How Instruments Work and How They Fail
## PPL_PA_I_G_05 | ACS Elements: PA.I.G.K1h, PA.I.G.K1i

**RKP_01: Pitot-Static — What Uses What**
ASI: uses BOTH pitot (ram) AND static pressure (differential = airspeed). ONLY instrument using pitot.
ALTIMETER: uses STATIC ONLY.
VSI: uses STATIC ONLY (rate of change through a calibrated bleed).

BLOCKED PITOT (static open, drain hole blocked): ASI FREEZES then OVER-READS in climb (trapped pitot pressure vs. falling static).
BLOCKED STATIC (pitot open): ASI, altimeter, and VSI all freeze at blockage altitude values. ASI UNDER-READS in climb.

**RKP_02: Pitot Heat and Alternate Static**
Pitot heat: PREVENTIVE — turn on BEFORE entering icing/IMC. Verify with ammeter increase (3-5A).
Ice on pitot = icing conditions → turn on carburetor heat simultaneously.
Alternate static: activates cockpit port. Effect: altimeter reads HIGHER than actual (cabin slightly lower pressure). ASI reads slightly high.
Must be accounted for in IMC approach minimums.

**RKP_03: Vacuum System and Partial Panel**
Vacuum pump → suction → spins gyro rotors in AI and HI.
Vacuum failure: AI and HI FAIL (may continue for minutes on residual spin before becoming erratic).
SURVIVES vacuum failure: Turn Coordinator (electrical gyro) + Magnetic Compass.
Partial panel: TC + altimeter + ASI + compass. TC shows rate of turn and bank direction.
Check suction gauge (4.5-5.5 in. Hg normal). If low → suspect gyro instruments.

**RKP_04: Compass Errors — ANDS and OSSC**
ACCELERATION ERROR (ANDS — northern hemisphere):
- Accelerate → compass shows NORTH
- Decelerate → compass shows SOUTH
(Worst on east/west headings; negligible on north/south)

TURNING ERROR:
- Rolling out TO NORTH → compass lags → roll out EARLY (undershoot)
- Rolling out TO SOUTH → compass leads → roll out LATE (overshoot)
Mnemonic: When turning to North, stop short. When turning to South, keep going.
HI (gyroscopic): no compass errors, but drifts — resync to compass every 15-20 min.

### Key Regulations
- 14 CFR 91.205 — Required instruments: pitot heat for flight in icing, airspeed, altimeter, and compass required for VFR/IFR
