# Lesson 32: Hypoxia, Hyperventilation, and Physiological Factors in Aviation
## PPL_PA_I_H_01 | ACS Elements: PA.I.H.K1, PA.I.H.K2

**RKP_01: Hypoxia — The Self-Diagnosis Paradox**
O2 percentage stays 21% at altitude — but PARTIAL PRESSURE drops. Less O2 molecules per breath.
4 types: Hypoxic (altitude), Hypemic (CO/anemia blocks hemoglobin), Stagnant (poor circulation), Histotoxic (drugs/alcohol block cell use).
PARADOX: the FIRST symptom is euphoria — feeling fine while performance crumbles. Pilots cannot self-diagnose.
Time of Useful Consciousness: 18,000 ft = 30 min. 25,000 ft = 3-5 min. 35,000 ft = under 1 min.
Regulations: 14 CFR 91.211: above 12,500 ft >30 min → crew uses O2. Above 14,000 ft → always on O2. Above 15,000 ft → passengers receive O2.
SOLUTION: apply O2 PROACTIVELY before symptoms appear. Do not wait.

**RKP_02: Hyperventilation — Mimic and Treatment**
Mechanism: breathing too fast → CO2 drops → blood becomes alkalotic → tingling, dizziness, tunnel vision.
The breathing reflex is triggered by CO2 NOT O2 — feel no air hunger.
vs. Hypoxia: hypoxia = euphoria, slow onset. Hyperventilation = anxiety, tingling, distress, acute.
Treatment: SLOW breathing deliberately. Talk out loud. Breathe into paper bag.
At altitude: RULE OUT HYPOXIA FIRST (apply O2 — if no help within 30-60 seconds, treat as hyperventilation).

**RKP_03: Carbon Monoxide — Heat Off, Ventilate, Land**
Source: cracked exhaust muffler → CO into cabin heat exchanger → cabin air.
CO binds hemoglobin 200-240x more than O2 → hypemic hypoxia.
DIAGNOSTIC SYMPTOM: dull headache that improves when vent opened, returns when closed.
Response: (1) Cabin heat OFF. (2) Windows/vents open for fresh air. (3) Supplemental O2 if available. (4) Declare emergency. (5) Land ASAP.
CO detector strip: yellow = safe, gray/black = CO detected. Check on every preflight.

**RKP_04: Scuba Diving Before Flying**
Dissolved nitrogen in tissues from scuba → altitude pressure drop → bubbles form (DCS).
FAA recommended wait times:
- Non-decompression dive: wait 8 HOURS minimum before flying.
- Decompression dive or multiple dives: wait 24 HOURS minimum.
DCS symptoms: joint pain (the bends), chest pain (the chokes), neuro symptoms (the staggers).
Treatment: descend, oxygen, seek immediate medical care (hyperbaric chamber).

### Key Regulations
- 14 CFR 91.211 — Supplemental oxygen requirements by altitude and duration
- 14 CFR 91.17 — Alcohol; drugs — impairment standards including physiological fitness
