# Lesson 24: Weight and Balance — Loading, CG Limits, and the Moment Method
## PPL_PA_I_F_02 | ACS Elements: PA.I.F.K2, PA.I.F.S1

### What This Lesson Covers
The legal framework for weight limits under 14 CFR 91.9, the moment method calculation (Weight × Arm = Moment), the critical difference between forward and aft CG conditions, and how CG shifts during flight as fuel burns in different tank locations.

### The Four Knowledge Points

**RKP_01: 14 CFR 91.9 — Weight and CG Limits Are Hard Lines**
Max gross weight: must not be exceeded at ANY point — including at takeoff before burn-down.
'I''ll burn into limits' is illegal: aircraft is overloaded during the most critical phase of flight.
CG limits: from the TCDS and POH. Must remain within limits for the ENTIRE flight — not just at takeoff.
Forward CG limit: pitch control authority. Aft CG limit: static stability.

**RKP_02: The Moment Method — Step by Step**
1. List every item: BEW, pilot, pax, fuel, baggage.
2. Find each ARM from the POH loading table.
3. Calculate MOMENT: Weight × Arm = Moment (pound-inches).
4. Sum all weights = Total Weight. Sum all moments = Total Moment.
5. CG = Total Moment ÷ Total Weight (in inches from datum).
6. Verify CG is within forward and aft limits at computed total weight.

Critical numbers: Avgas = 6 lb/gal. Jet-A = 6.7 lb/gal. Water = 8.33 lb/gal.
BEW (Basic Empty Weight) is ALWAYS the first row — forgetting it is the #1 calculation error.

**RKP_03: Forward vs. Aft CG — Different Failure Modes**
FORWARD CG (heavy nose):
- More stable, heavier controls, more back pressure needed
- Higher stall speed IAS, possible reduced flare authority
- Safer than aft CG for spin recovery

AFT CG (heavy tail):
- Reduced stability — can become UNSTABLE behind neutral point
- Lighter feel but abrupt/unpredictable stall
- Worst spin recovery characteristics — many aircraft unrecoverable aft of spin limit
- Most dangerous CG condition

**RKP_04: CG Shift During Flight**
Fuel burns → total weight decreases → CG shifts AWAY from tank location.
Tanks AFT of CG → burning fuel moves CG FORWARD. Tanks FORWARD of CG → CG drifts AFT as fuel burns.
Check: compute CG at both takeoff weight AND at zero-fuel weight. Both must be within limits.
Passenger briefing: no movement during critical phases — forward/aft passenger shift has real CG effect.

### Key Regulations
- 14 CFR 91.9 — Civil aircraft flight limitations: weight and CG limits (hard legal limits)
