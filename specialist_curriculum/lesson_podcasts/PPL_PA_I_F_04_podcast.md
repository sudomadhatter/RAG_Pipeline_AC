# Lesson 26: Aircraft Systems — Engine, Fuel, Pitot-Static, and Gyroscopic Instruments
## PPL_PA_I_F_04 | ACS Element: PA.I.F.K4

### What This Lesson Covers
The four-stroke engine cycle and carburetor icing (the most frequent piston failure), fuel types and the sump drain contamination test, pitot-static blockage scenarios (each instrument's specific behavior), and gyroscopic instruments (vacuum vs. electric, rigidity vs. precession, compass errors).

### The Four Knowledge Points

**RKP_01: Four-Stroke Cycle and Carburetor Ice**
Cycle: Intake → Compression → Power → Exhaust.
Carburetor ice: forms in the venturi due to temperature drop from fuel vaporization. OAT from 20°F to 70°F with humidity — NOT just cold weather.
Symptoms: gradual RPM drop (fixed), MP drop (CS prop), rough running.
Immediate action: CARBURETOR HEAT ON. Expect initial rougher running (melting ice), then smooth restoration.
Hot carb air reduces engine efficiency slightly — turn off carb heat once clear.

**RKP_02: Fuel — 100LL vs. Jet-A and the Sump Drain**
100LL Avgas: BLUE. Piston engines only.
Jet-A: CLEAR/STRAW. Turbines only. Misfueling a piston with Jet-A = immediate catastrophic engine failure.
Contamination: water sinks below avgas. Water in sample = drain until clear. If it persists, ground the aircraft.
Always drain: TANK SUMPS (all tanks) + FUEL STRAINER. Blue = avgas. Clear/straw in a piston = wrong fuel.

**RKP_03: Pitot-Static Blockage — What Each Instrument Does**
| Blockage | ASI | ALT | VSI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pitot tube blocked (drain open) | Zero or incorrect | Normal | Normal |
| Pitot tube + drain blocked | Freezes at last reading | Normal | Normal |
| Static port blocked | False (reads low in climb, high in descent) | Freezes | Freezes at zero |

Alternate static source: routes cabin air to static instruments. Slight error vs. outside but usable.
Pitot heat: ON in visible moisture below +5°C OAT.

**RKP_04: Gyroscopic Instruments — Vacuum vs. Electric**
RIGIDITY: spinning gyro resists change in orientation → stable attitude reference.
PRECESSION: force applied to gyro causes 90° reaction → explains AI drift in prolonged turns, HI drift over time.
VACUUM powered: Attitude Indicator + Heading Indicator.
ELECTRICALLY powered: Turn Coordinator (different power source — redundancy).
Vacuum failure: AI and HI fail. Turn Coordinator remains. Fly partial panel: TC + ALT + ASI + clock.
Compass errors: ANDS (Accelerate=North, Decelerate=South on E/W headings). TURNING ERRORS: leads in N, lags in S.
