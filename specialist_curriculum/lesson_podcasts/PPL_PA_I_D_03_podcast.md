# Lesson 17: VFR Flight Plans — Elements, Filing, Activating, and Closing
## PPL_PA_I_D_03 | ACS Elements: PA.I.D.K4, PA.I.D.K5

### What This Lesson Covers
The VFR flight plan is your search-and-rescue insurance policy. This lesson covers the nine required elements of 14 CFR 91.153, the four-step lifecycle (file → activate → amend → close), the three-source route verification system (sectional + Chart Supplement + NOTAMs), and AC 91-78A EFB risk management.

### The Four Knowledge Points

**RKP_01: The Nine Required Elements (91.153)**
1. Aircraft ID (tail number)
2. Type of flight plan (VFR)
3. Aircraft type, equipment, and wake category
4. True airspeed in knots
5. Departure point (ICAO identifier)
6. Proposed departure time (UTC)
7. Cruising altitude
8. Route of flight
9. Destination + ETE + alternate (if desired)
Plus: fuel on board in hours:minutes (defines SAR search radius — NOT the same as ETE), souls on board, pilot info, aircraft color.

THE SWAP TRAP: Item 19 = fuel on board (e.g., 0430 = 4 hrs 30 min of fuel). Item 16 = ETE (actual flight time). Swapping them tells SAR you ran out of fuel mid-route.

**RKP_02: The Four-Step Flight Plan Lifecycle**
FILING → puts plan in dormant queue. Expires 2 hours after proposed departure if not activated.
ACTIVATING → starts the SAR clock. Radio call on 122.2 MHz after takeoff. EFB apps can activate too.
AMENDING → if you will be 30+ minutes late to destination, call FSS. Failure = SAR triggered while you're still flying.
CLOSING → legally required (91.153). Call 1-800-WX-BRIEF immediately after landing. 30 minutes past ETA = SAR chain starts.

THE TOWER TRAP: VFR towers do NOT close VFR flight plans. Only FSS closes VFR plans. Landing at Atlanta ATL does not close your flight plan. YOU must call.

**RKP_03: Three-Source Route Verification**
Sectional → static overview (updated every 6 months). Use for terrain, airspace, MEFs, NAVAIDs.
Chart Supplement → airport details (updated every 56 days). Use for runway lengths, RP (right pattern flags), lighting, noise abatement.
NOTAMs → real-time dynamic data. Use for: runway closures, unlit towers, NAVAID outages, TFRs, GPS anomalies. Check as close to departure as possible.

NOTAMs supersede Chart Supplement data. Chart Supplement supersedes Sectional for operational details. SUA: call the controlling agency (listed in Chart Supplement) to verify MOA status before transiting.

**RKP_04: EFB Risk Management — AC 91-78A**
No FAA approval required for Part 91 VFR. Database must be current. Backup plan is mandatory.
Main failure modes: thermal shutdown (direct sun), battery death, GPS loss, software crash.
Mitigation: 100% battery at departure, near air vents, secondary device or paper charts, pilotage proficiency so you never fully depend on the magenta line.

The DPE test: 'Your iPad just died. Where are we?' → identify a landmark, place it on a paper sectional, continue visually.

### Key Regulations
- 14 CFR 91.153 — VFR flight plan required elements and closing obligation
- 14 CFR 91.103 — Preflight action (NOTAMs, Chart Supplement, route planning)
- 14 CFR 91.21 — Portable electronic device use
- AC 91-78A — Electronic Flight Bag operational guidance
