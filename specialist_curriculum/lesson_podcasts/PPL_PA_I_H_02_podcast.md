# Lesson 33: Spatial Disorientation, Illusions, and the Vestibular System
## PPL_PA_I_H_02 | ACS Elements: PA.I.H.K4, PA.I.H.K5

**RKP_01: The Leans — Vestibular Deception and the Trust Rule**
Semicircular canals: detect RATE of rotation. Cannot detect gradual bank below ~2°/sec threshold.
THE LEANS: undetected gradual bank → pilot corrects → brain perceives the CORRECTION as the motion → feels banked opposite when actually wings-level.
Dangerous: extremely convincing physical sensation. Persists 1-3 minutes.
ONLY CORRECT RESPONSE: TRUST THE INSTRUMENTS. Do not adjust aircraft toward the false sensation.
90% of spatial disorientation accidents are fatal. No instrument rating = no IMC operations.

**RKP_02: Graveyard Spiral — Why Pulling Back Makes It Worse**
Sequence: undetected bank → nose drops → pilot pulls back (without first leveling wings) → pulls TIGHTER into banked turn → airspeed increases → bank steepens → spiral tightens.
Why pulling back fails: in a bank, elevator creates MORE TURN, not level-offs.
CORRECT RECOVERY: LEVEL WINGS FIRST (AI or TC), THEN back pressure to stop descent.
VFR-into-IMC: average unrated pilot loses control within 178 seconds. Never enter IMC without IFR rating.

**RKP_03: Visual Approach Illusions**
NARROW runway → appears farther/lower → pilot tends too LOW (CFIT).
WIDE runway → appears closer/higher → pilot tends too HIGH.
UPSLOPING runway → appears higher than actual → pilot tends too LOW.
DOWNSLOPING runway → appears lower than actual → pilot tends too HIGH.
NIGHT / BLACK HOLE → no terrain reference → typically fly too LOW.
MITIGATION: VASI/PAPI is authoritative. Two red, two white = on glidepath. Trust it over visual impression.

**RKP_04: Somatogravic Illusion — The Go-Around Trap**
Full power acceleration → otolith interprets forward G as pitch-UP signal.
Pilot pushes forward to correct imagined excessive nose-high → actual pitch-DOWN during low-altitude go-around → CFIT.
PREVENTION: at night/IMC go-around, trust attitude indicator. RESIST push-forward instinct.
All illusions share one solution: TRUST THE INSTRUMENTS. The vestibular system was not designed for flight.
