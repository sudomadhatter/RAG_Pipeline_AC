# Lesson 20: Class D, E, and G Airspace
## PPL_PA_I_E_02 | ACS Elements: PA.I.E.K1d, PA.I.E.K1e, PA.I.E.K1f

### What This Lesson Covers
The lower three airspace classes that VFR pilots spend most of their time in: Class D (the tower-based class with the part-time trap), Class E (the default controlled airspace nobody fully understands), and Class G (uncontrolled with the day/night minimum switch). Plus sectional chart symbology to identify them visually.

### The Four Knowledge Points

**RKP_01: Class D — Tower Contact, Not Clearance. And the Part-Time Trap.**
4 NM radius (typical), surface to 2,500 AGL. Dashed blue circle on sectional.
Entry: two-way radio established (callsign acknowledged — same rule as Class C). No Mode C/ADS-B requirement beyond general rules.
Minimums: 3 SM, 500 below/1,000 above/2,000 horizontal.
PART-TIME TRAP: When the tower closes, Class D becomes Class E or Class G based on the Chart Supplement — not a predictable default. Check the Chart Supplement before flying near towered airports at night or on holidays.

**RKP_02: Class E — No Radio Required, But It IS Controlled Airspace**
Class E starts at: 1,200 AGL (default, where magenta fading ends on sectional), OR 700 AGL (solid magenta shading boundary), OR the surface (dashed magenta boundary = Class E surface area protecting IFR approaches at untowered airports).
Entry: none required. No radio, no transponder needed for VFR entry.
Minimums BELOW 10,000 MSL: 3 SM, 500 below/1,000 above/2,000 horizontal.
Minimums AT OR ABOVE 10,000 MSL: 5 SM, 1,000 below/1,000 above/1 SM horizontal.
The 10,000 jump exists because aircraft are faster at altitude — closure rates are higher, requiring more visibility buffer.

**RKP_03: Class G — Uncontrolled. Know the Day vs. Night Switch.**
Uncontrolled from surface to base of Class E (700 or 1,200 AGL typically). No entry requirements, no communication requirements.
Day minimums:
  - At or below 1,200 AGL: 1 SM, clear of clouds
  - Above 1,200 AGL (below 10,000 MSL): 1 SM, 500/1,000/2,000
Night minimums:
  - At or below 1,200 AGL: 3 SM, clear of clouds ← THE JUMP
  - Above 1,200 AGL (below 10,000 MSL): 3 SM, 500/1,000/2,000

**RKP_04: Reading the Sectional — Match the Symbol to the Class**
Blue solid (thick) = Class B shelves
Blue segmented circle = Class C (5 and 10 NM rings)
Blue dashed circle = Class D
Dashed magenta boundary = Class E surface area
Solid magenta shading/fade = Class E from 700 AGL
No shading + below E floor = Class G
Blue airport icon = controlled tower. Magenta airport icon = uncontrolled.
